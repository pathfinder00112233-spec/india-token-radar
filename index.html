Audit Methodology

Audit Methodology

How entries are sourced, classified, reviewed, and presented in this independent registry.

Source hierarchy

Sources are prioritized in this order: regulator publications and sandbox registries, issuer or exchange disclosures, and reputable financial reporting where primary documentation is not publicly available.

Status discipline

Statuses reflect the documented state represented by the cited evidence. Announcements, consultations, sandbox approvals, trials, pilots, operational systems, and withdrawn applications are not treated as interchangeable categories.

Scope

The registry covers tokenization, DLT market infrastructure, CBDC pilots, regulatory-sandbox initiatives, and closely related financial infrastructure.

Review dates

Each record carries a registry review date indicating when the entry was last maintained in this interface. A review date does not by itself mean that every underlying source document was re-issued, changed, or independently re-verified on that date.

Important scope note

Not every entry represents a tokenized asset. The registry also tracks relevant DLT infrastructure, CBDC pilots, digital financial-market infrastructure, and regulatory-sandbox activity.

Research integrity

Entries are included because supporting public evidence was identified. No initiative is added merely to fill a row, create a numerical target, or imply regulatory activity that cannot be substantiated. Where primary documentation is unavailable, the source type is explicitly identified.

Source-context notes

Important contextual events may be attached to individual records, including sandbox status, limited-use authorization, application withdrawal, secondary-source dependence, or regulatory action.
